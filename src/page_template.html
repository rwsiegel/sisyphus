How the score works

Each open data role is scored out of : pay (does the top of the range reach __PAY_FLOOR__), role fit (analytics engineer scores highest, then data engineer, data platform, software engineer on a data platform; seniority; Boston or remote US), recency (1 day or less scores highest, easing down to 90 days), hiring (how many roles the company has open), investors, and layoffs and a location outside Boston and remote US subtract. Nothing is dropped: a role that fails a criterion just scores low, and the filters let you hide it. A company's number is its best role's score compared with the average. A near miss is a role that misses exactly one hard criterion by a small margin: posted 8 to 30 days ago, or pay close to the target. Stage outside Series A to D, industry, and a Glassdoor rating under 3.5 are flags, never reasons to drop a company. Facts come from public job boards and web research; each card lists its sources.

Adjust the scoring (dials)

Every weight is a dial from 0 to 2 times its default. The list re-ranks as you move one. Changes last until you reload the page.
